Privacy

This page describes how information sent through this website is treated. It is a holding draft until counsel reviews it.

[NEEDS: legal review before publish]

Who this covers

The site is published for the Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (display name ASHA). It is used by member hotels, prospective members, government officers, and the public.

What the forms ask for

The contact form may collect a name, hotel or organisation, role, email, optional phone, and a message. The membership form may also collect category, star classification, city, and district. A hidden field is present only to discourage automated submissions.

How that information is used

Information is collected so the secretariat can respond to an enquiry or review an application. It is not shared for marketing. Delivery to the secretariat, and any confirmation email, wait until a mail service is connected.
