Andhra Pradesh Tourism / Tourism Information Notice

The festival dates below are drawn directly from the official State 2025–26 tourism calendar published by the Department of Tourism, Government of Andhra Pradesh. These events are organized by state and district authorities, not by ASHA. They are presented here as an operational reference for member hotel occupancy planning and staffing across the 26 districts.

  1. 5-8 June 2025

    Masula Beach Festival

    Manginapudi Beach, Machilipatnam. Beach sports, cultural performances, handicrafts, and food stalls.

  2. 17-19 September 2025

    AP Travel Mart

    Visakhapatnam. Trade meeting for the State's tourism calendar.

  3. 22 September-2 October 2025

    Vijayawada Utsav

    Classical and folk arts, Krishna riverfront activity, heritage walks, and night bazaars.

  4. 24 September-2 October 2025

    Sri Venkateswara Swamy Brahmotsavam

    Tirumala. Listed on the State tourism calendar.

  5. 14-16 November 2025

    Amaravati Diaries

    Literary and music festival, Vijayawada.

  6. 8-10 January 2026

    Avakai Amaravati Festival

    Vijayawada. Listed on the State festival calendar.

  7. 24 January-1 February 2026

    Visakha Utsav

    Visakhapatnam and nearby districts, including Anakapalli Utsav and Araku Utsav.

  8. 31 January-2 February 2026

    Araku Balloon and Chali Festival

    Araku Valley. Listed on the State tourism calendar.
