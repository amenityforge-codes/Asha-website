Membership

Eligibility to join ASHA, and the ways hotels and partners can apply.

Eligibility to join ASHA

A hotel may apply if it is classified as three-star and above and operates in Andhra Pradesh. The Association's authorised territory is the twenty-six districts of the State.

Industry partners are suppliers, consultants, and service providers. Institutional partners are tourism bodies, universities, and associations.

Ways to join

Choose the category that matches your organisation, then submit the application on this page. The secretariat can also receive an enquiry through Contact.

Hotel Members
Three-star and above hotels in Andhra Pradesh.
Industry Partners
Suppliers, consultants, and service providers to the hospitality sector.
Institutional Partners
Tourism bodies, universities, and associations.

Benefits

Receive coordinated regulatory updates, representation on common industry matters, access to training programmes, participation in government consultations and visibility through ASHA's tourism-promotion initiatives.

Secretariat & Accreditation

  • Liaison Headquarters

    Vijayawada / Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh

  • Classification Eligibility

    Hotels classified 3-Star and above by statutory classification authorities operating within Andhra Pradesh.

  • Credential Verification

    Submitted property details and operating classifications are reviewed by the secretariat prior to formal induction into the accredited member registry.

  • Direct Enquiries

    For urgent questions regarding documentation, partnership categories, or association liaison, please visit the Contact Page.

Either the President or the General Secretary is ordinarily based in Vijayawada or Amaravati for active state government liaison.
