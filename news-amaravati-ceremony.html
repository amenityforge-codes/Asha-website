ASHA Representatives at the Amaravati Foundation Stone-Laying Ceremony

Association: Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) Verified Date: 28 November 2025 Location: Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh Chief Guest: Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman, Union Finance Minister

Ceremony Overview & Factual Summary

Representatives of the Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) attended the foundation stone-laying ceremony for the permanent offices of 15 public sector banks and insurance companies in Amaravati on November 28, 2025.

The foundation stone-laying ceremony was held in the presence of Chief Guest Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman, Hon'ble Union Minister for Finance & Corporate Affairs, alongside Hon'ble Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh N. Chandrababu Naidu, senior ministers, and chairpersons and executives of public sector financial institutions.

During the event, association representatives attended the proceedings and presented an official memorandum concerning hospitality and commercial development in the capital region and statewide star hotel operations.

The development of permanent headquarters for 15 financial institutions in Amaravati marks a strategic institutional milestone for the State capital, expanding corporate travel, commercial infrastructure, and institutional hospitality demand across Andhra Pradesh.
