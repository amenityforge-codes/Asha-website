About the Association

Established for the organised hotel sector.

The Association exists to represent star hotels across Andhra Pradesh and to strengthen the organised hotel sector.

26districts.
one unified voice.

01 / Foundation

Core purpose

ASHA exists so star hotels across Andhra Pradesh have a single voice. It works for growth, collaboration, professional standards, and the sustainable development of the organised hotel sector.

The Association is a platform for member hotels, government departments, Andhra Pradesh Tourism, local authorities, industry partners, and hospitality institutions.

Everything ASHA does is for the collective benefit of the sector and the elevation of hospitality standards across all twenty-six districts.

02 / Strategic Focus

How ASHA serves the industry

A focused platform bringing together hotel leadership, state policymakers, and tourism stakeholders.

Unified Policy Advocacy

ASHA represents star hotels before state and central government bodies, Andhra Pradesh Tourism authorities, municipal corporations, and regulators on policies, ease of doing business, and sector-wide concerns.

Standards & Operational Excellence

Advancing service quality, safety protocols, sustainable practices, and operational benchmarks across three-star and above hospitality properties throughout the State.

Destination Andhra Pradesh

Partnering with tourism leadership to position Andhra Pradesh as a premier destination for business, heritage, pilgrimage, coastal, and eco-travel.
