About the Association

Established for the organised hotel sector.

The Association exists to represent star hotels across Andhra Pradesh and to strengthen the organised hotel sector.

one unified voice.

01 / Foundation

Core purpose

ASHA exists so star hotels across Andhra Pradesh have a single voice. It works for growth, collaboration, professional standards, and the sustainable development of the organised hotel sector.

The Association is a platform for member hotels, government departments, Andhra Pradesh Tourism, local authorities, industry partners, and training institutions.

Everything ASHA does is for the collective benefit of the sector. The clause-numbered wording is on Governing Documents.

02 / Principle

Governing character

ASHA is a non-profit association. Income and assets are used for its stated purposes, not for private profit.

Any commercial activity the Association may lawfully undertake is incidental to those purposes, and the proceeds stay with them. The full governing character is on Governing Documents.
