# Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh

A non-profit society representing three-star and above hotels across all 26 districts of Andhra Pradesh. The public site exists so members, government officers, tourism bodies, and the press can read the Association’s mission, aims, objectives, and non-profit character without opening the Memorandum.

**Audience:** member hotels, prospective members, Andhra Pradesh Tourism and other departments, local authorities, and industry partners.

**Purpose:** explain who the Association is, what it is authorised to do, and how it operates on a non-profit basis.

**Voice:** institutional, precise, unhurried. Governing-document language is quoted, not paraphrased into marketing slogans.

**Constraints:** no private-profit implication; clause numbers stay visible because they map to the Memorandum; display name may later be replaced by the registered legal title.

**Mode by surface:** Home and Contact persuade/invite; Mission, About, Objectives, and Governing Text are for reading.
