Aims, Mission and Objectives of the Association

3.1 Establishment and Core Purpose

The Association is established as a non-profit society to promote the growth, collaboration, representation, professional excellence, and sustainable development of hotels classified as three-star and above across all twenty-six (26) districts of the State of Andhra Pradesh.

The Association shall serve as a unified institutional platform representing the hospitality industry and facilitating cooperation among member hotels, government departments, Andhra Pradesh Tourism authorities, local authorities, industry stakeholders, educational and training institutions, national and international hospitality organisations, and other relevant public and private institutions.

All activities of the Association shall be carried out on a non-profit basis, solely in furtherance of the Mission, Aims, and Objectives set out herein, and not for the private profit or personal gain of any member.

3.2 Mission

To represent, strengthen, and advance three-star and above hotels across Andhra Pradesh by promoting hospitality excellence, high standards of service, hygiene, food safety, sustainability, and guest and operational safety; advocating tourism-friendly public policy; fostering professional development; and positioning Andhra Pradesh as a premier tourism and hospitality destination.

3.3 Aims

The Aims of the Association, which give effect to the Mission, are as follows:

3.3.1

To support and represent member hotels in matters of public policy, investment, tourism development, industry regulation, and public engagement.

3.3.2

To provide a unified platform for structured collaboration among government authorities, private stakeholders, educational institutions, and the hospitality industry.

3.3.3

To uphold and promote benchmarks of service quality, hygiene, food safety, guest and operational safety, professionalism, and sustainability.

3.3.4

To promote Andhra Pradesh as a premier tourism and hospitality destination through advocacy, training, capacity-building, recognition, and public engagement.

3.3.5

To foster the growth, collaboration, professional excellence, and sustainable development of the organised hotel sector throughout the State.

3.4 Non-Profit Character

3.4.1

The Association is constituted and shall function exclusively on a non-profit basis.

3.4.2

The income, property, funds, and assets of the Association shall be applied solely towards the attainment of its Aims and Objectives.

3.4.3

No portion of the income or property of the Association shall be paid, transferred, or distributed, directly or indirectly, by way of dividend, bonus, profit, or otherwise, to any member, office-bearer, or other person, except as reasonable remuneration, reimbursement, or consideration for services actually rendered, or expenses actually incurred, in furtherance of the Association’s Objectives, and in accordance with its governing documents and applicable laws and regulations.

3.4.4

Any activity of a commercial, trading, or revenue-generating nature that the Association may lawfully undertake shall be incidental or ancillary to the Objectives herein, and the proceeds thereof shall be applied exclusively to the Association’s non-profit purposes.

3.5 Objectives

In furtherance of the Mission and Aims, and not otherwise, the Association shall have the following Objectives. The same text is arranged for navigation on the Objectives page.

3.5.1 Representation and Advocacy. Represent three-star and above hotels across all 26 districts; appear before government, tourism authorities, regulators, and policy institutions; liaise with public and private stakeholders; advocate for tourism, hospitality, investment, employment, and infrastructure; maintain a structured government-industry platform; and ensure the President or General Secretary is ordinarily based in Vijayawada or Amaravati (3.5.1.1-3.5.1.6).

3.5.2 Quality, Standards and Professional Excellence. Promote high standards of service; encourage hygiene, sanitation, food safety, guest safety, security, sustainability, and professionalism; establish or promote benchmarks and best practices; facilitate knowledge-sharing; and encourage responsible hospitality (3.5.2.1-3.5.2.5).

3.5.3 Member Development, Training and Recognition. Organise training, seminars, conferences, and workshops; cover operations, management, marketing, technology, compliance, sustainability, food safety, innovation, and human resources; encourage continuous professional development; recognise outstanding hotels and professionals; and provide showcase opportunities (3.5.3.1-3.5.3.5).

3.5.4 Crisis Management and Member Support. Represent and support members during disasters, pandemics, emergencies, regulatory change, and economic disruption; coordinate collective responses where lawful; facilitate communication with government and relief agencies; and share guidance and resources (3.5.4.1-3.5.4.4).

3.5.5 Tourism, Industry Promotion and Public Engagement. Promote Andhra Pradesh as a premier destination; represent the sector nationally and internationally; participate in fairs and forums; engage media; promote the positive image of member hotels and their contribution to employment and the economy; and publish promotional and educational materials (3.5.5.1-3.5.5.6).

3.5.6 Strategic Institutional Affiliations. Obtain and maintain memberships and partnerships with State, national, and international bodies, subject to law and the Association’s non-profit character; participate in federations and chambers; use affiliations to advance members’ legitimate interests; and keep all collaborations consistent with the Objectives (3.5.6.1-3.5.6.4).

3.5.7 Educational, Institutional and Industry Development. Establish or support charitable institutions, libraries, and similar activities; hospitality and vocational training institutions; skill development and employment; food-quality and hygiene laboratories subject to licences; and research and technological development (3.5.7.1-3.5.7.5).

3.5.8 Publications, Communication and Publicity. Publish journals, newsletters, reports, and research; disseminate information on hospitality, tourism, regulations, and policy; organise press conferences and public-awareness programmes; and maintain communication with members, government, and the public (3.5.8.1-3.5.8.4).

3.5.9 Exhibitions, Conferences and Industry Events. Organise or participate in exhibitions of hospitality products and services; organise conferences, conventions, and workshops; and provide platforms for discussion of policy, regulation, technology, investment, operations, and tourism (3.5.9.1-3.5.9.3).

3.5.10 Regional and Branch Development. Establish branch, regional, or chapter offices across Andhra Pradesh as required; ensure representation from different regions and districts; and promote balanced development of the State’s hospitality sector (3.5.10.1-3.5.10.3).

3.5.11 Property, Assets and Financial Resources. Acquire and deal with property required for the Objectives; establish offices, training facilities, laboratories, and libraries; invest funds only as permitted by law and solely for non-profit objects; raise funds through lawful means; borrow where legally permissible; and manage or encumber Association property in accordance with law and the governing documents (3.5.11.1-3.5.11.6).

3.5.12 General and Incidental Objectives. Undertake any lawful activity incidental to the Aims; enter into lawful agreements with public or private institutions; undertake trade, education, tourism, research, or training activities that directly support the Objectives and remain non-profit; and take all other lawful measures necessary for the Aims, provided no measure confers private profit upon any member (3.5.12.1-3.5.12.4).
