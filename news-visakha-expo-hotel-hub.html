Visakhapatnam Set to Emerge as Premier Hotel Hub with 16 Five-Star Properties: AP Chambers Business Expo 2026

At the AP Chambers Business Expo 2026 in Visakhapatnam, Tourism Minister Kandula Durgesh, Union Civil Aviation Minister Ram Mohan Naidu, MLA Ganta Srinivasa Rao, and ASHA President R. V. Swamy highlighted the rapid expansion of Visakhapatnam's hotel sector, projected 16 upcoming five-star properties, and launched "The Godavari Calls - Pushkaram 2027" campaign.

Source: Surya Telugu Daily (సూర్య) · Page 3 Date: 12 September 2026 Venue: Gadiraju Palace, Beach Road, Visakhapatnam Organiser: AP Chambers of Commerce and Industry Federation

Executive Briefing & State Infrastructure Projection

Visakhapatnam, the executive and economic powerhouse of Andhra Pradesh, is set to undergo a transformative expansion in luxury hospitality. Speaking at the inauguration of the 3-day AP Chambers Business Expo 2026 at Gadiraju Palace on Beach Road, Visakhapatnam, Hon'ble Minister for Tourism, Culture and Cinematography Kandula Durgesh and Bheemili MLA Ganta Srinivasa Rao announced that 16 new five-star hotels will soon be operational across the coastal city.

ASHA President R. V. Swamy represented the Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh on the inaugural dais, joining state and central ministers to emphasize the strategic role of classified star hotels in generating thousands of direct jobs, driving MICE conventions, and supporting corporate expansion linked to the upcoming Bhogapuram International Airport.

Key Projections from Surya Newspaper Report:

  • 16 Upcoming Five-Star Hotels: High-end luxury properties under grounding through the State Investment Promotion Board (SIPB) along Sagar Nagar, Endada, and the Bhogapuram corridor.
  • Thousands of Hospitality Careers: Direct and indirect employment across culinary arts, hotel operations, tourism transport, local sourcing, and event management.
  • ₹50,000 Crore Investment Goal: Minister Kandula Durgesh articulated the state's vision to attract ₹50,000 crore in investments over the next 5 years under MSME & Entrepreneurship Policy 4.0.

Launch of "The Godavari Calls - Pushkaram 2027" Campaign

A highlight of the conclave was the official unveiling of Andhra Pradesh Tourism's flagship campaign: "The Godavari Calls — Pushkaram 2027".

Hon'ble Union Minister of Civil Aviation Kinjarapu Ram Mohan Naidu and Tourism Minister Kandula Durgesh unveiled the campaign alongside ASHA President R. V. Swamy and leading hospitality operators. The campaign initiates proactive planning across star hotels, airports, and regional transport infrastructure to ensure world-class guest accommodation and logistics for the millions of pilgrims and tourists expected during the Maha Godavari Pushkarams in 2027.

AP Chambers Business Expo Overview

The 3-day expo, organized by the AP Chambers of Commerce and Industry Federation under the theme "Connect – Build – Grow", featured over 180 stalls, 60+ new corporate enterprises, and 50 financial institutions, drawing over 50,000 business visitors. Alongside the trade exhibition, the official North Andhra Business Directory was released to foster B2B linkages between hospitality operators, local suppliers, and industrial corporations.
