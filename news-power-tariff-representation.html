AP Hotels Seek Reduction in High Power Tariff and Municipal Tax Relief

Hoteliers operating star hotels across Andhra Pradesh have called on the State Government to reduce commercial power tariffs from ₹12.90 to ₹7 per unit, extend power concessions to existing properties, and provide municipal tax relief.

Source: Deccan Chronicle (Sampat G. Samritan | DC) Date: 16 July 2025 Location: Vijayawada / Visakhapatnam / Kakinada Scope: ~80 Classified Star Hotels Statewide

Executive Briefing & Industry Appeal

Hoteliers operating classified star hotels across Andhra Pradesh have convened to call for an urgent reduction in the commercial power tariff of ₹12.90 per unit currently being levied on them. With monthly electricity bills running between ₹18 lakh and ₹20 lakh per property, energy costs have become an unsustainable financial burden that directly undermines hotel viability and operational quality.

As reported by Deccan Chronicle (Vijayawada dateline, July 16, 2025), the Star Hotels Association emphasized that Andhra Pradesh aims to promote tourism in a significant manner to attract domestic and international travelers. Because tourists prefer star hotels for their comfort, security, and quality of service, creating an enabling operating cost environment is vital to the State's tourism vision.

Statement by ASHA President R. V. Swamy:

“We appeal to the state government to provide some relief in power tariff and in municipal tax to help us provide quality service to the tourists and contribute our share for promotion of tourism in the state.”

Core Representations Submitted to the State Government

  1. Tariff Reduction to ₹7 per unit: Star hotels urged the Government of Andhra Pradesh to bring power tariffs down to ₹7 per unit, aligning Andhra Pradesh with competitive rates levied in other major tourism and industrial states such as Tamil Nadu and Punjab.
  2. Extending Power Concessions to Existing Hotels: While the State Government has accorded industry status to the hospitality sector, only newly constructed hotels currently receive the power concession of ₹2.30 per unit. ASHA has formally appealed for this ₹2.30/unit concession to be extended to existing hotels as well to provide equitable relief.
  3. Caution Deposit Rationalisation: Current regulations require hotels to maintain a caution deposit equivalent to two months of average electricity consumption with the electricity department. For a hotel with an average monthly bill of ₹20 lakh, ₹40 lakh remains locked up as security, severely constraining operational liquidity and capital renovation reserves.
  4. Mitigating Arbitrary Power Disconnections: Association members highlighted that temporary billing alerts or payment processing delays have led line staff to physically disconnect fuses at electric poles, abruptly forcing properties onto generator backup and causing severe embarrassment in front of domestic and international guests.
  5. 50% Concession on Annual Municipal Taxes: Member properties pay steep annual municipal property taxes ranging from ₹16 lakh to ₹18 lakh per hotel. ASHA has sought a 50% discount on these municipal levies.

Appreciation for Bar Excise Duty Reduction

The hoteliers formally thanked the State Government for a major reform previously enacted: reducing the annual excise duty on bars in star hotels from ₹66 lakh down to ₹25 lakh per unit. This progressive reduction was cited as a prime example of positive state intervention that revitalized licensed food and beverage operations.

Statewide Industry Mobilisation

Representing around 80 classified star hotels across Andhra Pradesh — including leading properties such as Novotel, Dolphin, Daspalla, Hyatt Place, GRT Grand, and Venky Residency — the Association convened regional roundtables at Dasapalla in Visakhapatnam and Sarovar Portico in Kakinada to consolidate representations and liaise with district administrations and the Energy Department.

Official Print Media Coverage

Source: Deccan Chronicle · Vijayawada Edition · Published July 16, 2025 · By Sampat G. Samritan | DC

Detailed report by Deccan Chronicle documenting ASHA's appeal to the Government of Andhra Pradesh for rationalisation of the ₹12.90/unit power tariff down to ₹7/unit, parity for existing hotels under industry status, and relief in municipal taxation.

Highlights from Deccan Chronicle Publication:
  • ₹7 Per Unit Tariff Sought: Benchmarked against tariffs in Tamil Nadu and Punjab.
  • Existing Hotels Relief: Requesting ₹2.30/unit power concession granted to new units to be extended to existing properties.
  • Excise Duty Reform Acknowledged: Acknowledged state reduction of annual bar excise duty in star hotels from ₹66 lakh to ₹25 lakh.
  • Municipal Tax Concession: 50% discount sought on annual municipal taxes averaging ₹16–18 lakh.
