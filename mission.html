01 / Our north star

Mission statement

“
ASHA represents star hotels across Andhra Pradesh. It works for better service, safety, and professional standards; for tourism-friendly public policy; and for the State as a hospitality destination.

02 / Objectives

Stronger together. Better represented. Ready for the future.

Running a hotel involves far more than delivering excellent guest experiences. It also means navigating regulations, developing skilled teams, adapting to industry change and ensuring that hospitality receives the attention it deserves in public policy. By joining ASHA, your hotel becomes part of a unified platform representing star hotels across the State.

A collective voice

ASHA represents the shared interests of member hotels before government departments, tourism authorities, regulators and other public institutions.

Timely industry support

Members receive relevant information on policies, regulations, compliance requirements and developments affecting the hospitality sector.

Training and professional development

Access workshops, seminars and knowledge-sharing programmes covering hotel operations, service standards, food safety, technology, sustainability, marketing, human resources and regulatory compliance.

Valuable industry connections

Build meaningful relationships with hotel owners, industry leaders, government representatives, educational institutions and tourism stakeholders from across Andhra Pradesh.

Tourism promotion and visibility

Participate in initiatives that promote Andhra Pradesh as a leading tourism destination.

Support during industry challenges

During regulatory changes, emergencies or major disruptions, ASHA provides a platform for coordinated communication, guidance and collective representation.

Shape the future of hospitality

Members can contribute to industry consultations, share regional concerns, exchange best practices and help influence initiatives.

Establishment & governing character

3.1 Establishment and Core Purpose

The Association is established to promote the growth, collaboration, representation, professional excellence, and sustainable development of hotels classified as three-star and above across all twenty-six (26) districts of the State of Andhra Pradesh.

The Association shall serve as a unified institutional platform representing the hospitality industry and facilitating cooperation among member hotels, government departments, Andhra Pradesh Tourism authorities, local authorities, industry stakeholders, educational and training institutions, national and international hospitality organisations, and other relevant public and private institutions.

All activities of the Association shall be carried out solely in furtherance of the Mission, Aims, and Objectives set out herein and for the collective advancement of the sector.

3.4 Governing Character

3.4.1

The Association is constituted and shall function exclusively in furtherance of its stated Aims and Objectives.

3.4.2

The income, property, funds, and assets of the Association shall be applied solely towards the attainment of its Aims and Objectives.

3.4.3

The income and property of the Association shall be administered in accordance with its governing documents and applicable laws and regulations.

3.4.4

Any activity of a commercial, trading, or revenue-generating nature that the Association may lawfully undertake shall be incidental or ancillary to the Objectives herein, and the proceeds thereof shall be applied exclusively to the Association’s stated purposes.
