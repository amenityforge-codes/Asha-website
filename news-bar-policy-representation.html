Government Engagement / Excise & Bar Policy Reform

ASHA Presents Key Recommendations for Progressive Bar Policy to Foster Tourism & MICE

A leadership delegation from the Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association met with Prohibition and Excise Commissioner Nishant Kumar, IAS, and Principal Secretary Mukesh Kumar Meena, IAS, presenting key recommendations to strengthen the bar policy and bolster MICE tourism.

Source: The Hans India (Hans News Service, Vijayawada) Date: 23 July 2025 Location: Vijayawada / Andhra Pradesh Authority: Department of Prohibition & Excise, Government of Andhra Pradesh

Executive Briefing & Consultation Scope

A delegation from the Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association (ASHA) held high-level consultations in Vijayawada with Excise and Prohibition Commissioner Nishant Kumar, IAS, and Principal Secretary (Excise & Prohibition) Mukesh Kumar Meena, IAS, to present strategic recommendations for strengthening the State's upcoming bar policy framework.

As reported by The Hans India, the primary objective of the engagement was to foster a collaborative approach between hospitality industry stakeholders and the Excise and Prohibition Department to formulate a progressive, modern bar policy that benefits both the hospitality sector and the State exchequer.

Delegation Representation

The ASHA leadership delegation was led by principal executive office bearers:

  • R. V. Swamy: President, ASHA & Chairman, Hyatt Place Vijayawada
  • S. Murthy Chittoory (Chitturi): General Secretary, ASHA & Joint MD, Venky Residency Kakinada
  • Sri Siva Kumar (Gudisa Shivakumar): Treasurer, ASHA & MD, SVN Lake Palace, Vizianagaram
  • Pawan Karthik (M. V. Pavan Kartheek): Joint Secretary, ASHA & MD, Hotel Elegant, Visakhapatnam
  • Sharan Valluru: Executive Committee Member, ASHA & MD, GRT Grand, Vijayawada
  • Participating Member Hoteliers: Representing classified star hotels across Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Tirupati, and Guntur.

Key Policy Recommendations Submitted

The delegation submitted structured recommendations aimed at positioning Andhra Pradesh as a premier destination for business conferences, leisure travelers, and destination weddings:

  1. Single-Bottle Indenting for Premium & Rare Brands: Permitting classified star hotels to place indents for single bottles of premium, ultra-premium, and imported spirits via simplified label registration, ensuring world-class cellars without requiring full-case commitments of slow-moving niche labels.
  2. APSBCL Depot Flexibility: Allowing star hotels to procure stocks from any authorized Andhra Pradesh State Beverages Corporation Limited (APSBCL) depot across the State, preventing supply bottlenecks during major international summits, MICE conferences, and corporate events.
  3. Reintroduction of Draft Beer & Microbreweries: Authorizing keg supply and draft beer dispensing in classified star hotels through APSBCL-approved distribution to match international hospitality standards.
  4. 24-Hour Time-Bound Event Approvals: Establishing a streamlined, time-bound digital approval mechanism allowing member properties to obtain event bar licenses within 24 hours for corporate conventions and destination banquets.
  5. Premium Domestic & International Brand Availability: Expanding the catalogue of registered premium labels to cater to domestic corporate executives, NRI visitors, and international tourists.

The delegation commended the proactive and receptive approach demonstrated by Commissioner Nishant Kumar, IAS, and Principal Secretary Mukesh Kumar Meena, IAS, assuring the department of the full cooperation of classified star hotels in achieving regulatory compliance, transparency, and sustainable revenue growth.
