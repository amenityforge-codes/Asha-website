Top Hoteliers Unite, Form ASHA, to Strengthen Hospitality Sector in A.P.

The association aims to elevate the State as a premier travel destination, promote excellence in hospitality, and foster collaborations with top-tier hotels.

Source: The Hindu (The Hindu Bureau) Date: 4 June 2025 Time: Published: June 04, 2025 11:17 PM IST | Updated: June 05, 2025 06:23 AM IST Location: Vijayawada / Andhra Pradesh

Foundation Scope & Official Assembly Record

Senior hoteliers from across Andhra Pradesh have come together to form the Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association (ASHA). The association aims to elevate the State as a premier travel destination, promote excellence in hospitality, and foster collaborations with top-tier hotels throughout the State's 26 districts.

The foundational assembly brought together prominent owners, managing directors, and general managers of classified three-star, four-star, five-star, and heritage hotels spanning Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Kakinada, Vizianagaram, Guntur, and Rajamahendravaram.

Elected Leadership Body of ASHA

The inaugural assembly unanimously ratified the election of key office bearers to steer the executive administration and liaison functions:

  • President: R. V. Swamy (Chairman, Hyatt Place Vijayawada)
  • General Secretary: S. Murthy Chittoory (Joint Managing Director, Venky Residency Kakinada)
  • Treasurer: Gudisa Shivakumar (Managing Director, SVN Lake Palace, Vizianagaram)
  • Vice-Presidents: Bala Krishna Reddy (Managing Director, Taj Bliss, Tirupati) & Ghattamaneni Venkat (Joint Managing Director, Daspalla Group, Vizag)
  • Joint Secretary: M. V. Pavan Kartheek (MD of Elegant, Vizag)
  • Executive Committee Members: Pandu Chowdhary (MD, Shelton and La hospin, Rajamahendravaram), Sharan Valluru (MD, GRT Grand, Vijayawada), Pratap N. (MD, Gowtham Grand in Guntur), Nageswara Rao (General Manager, Taj Vivanta, Vijayawada), and S. Prashant (General Manager, Daspalla Hotels).

Distinguished Advisory Board

To provide strategic counsel, sectoral foresight, and institutional guidance, an apex Advisory Board was constituted comprising stalwarts of India's hospitality sector:

  • Prabhu Kishore: Chairman of Novotel (Varun Hospitality)
  • Gokaraju Gangaraju: Chairman of Lemon Tree Premiere
  • M. Rajaiah: Chairman of Taj Vivanta
  • Muttavarapu Murali Krishna: Managing Director of Fortune Murali Park

Official National Media Coverage

Source: The Hindu · Andhra Pradesh Section · Published June 04, 2025 11:17 PM IST · Updated June 05, 2025 06:23 AM IST

National coverage published by The Hindu announcing the unified coalition of senior hoteliers forming ASHA and ratifying the state executive leadership and advisory board.

Institutional Objectives Confirmed by The Hindu:
  1. Destination Andhra Pradesh: Elevating the State as a premier international and national travel destination.
  2. Hospitality Excellence: Promoting benchmarks of professional excellence, sustainability, and service quality across member properties.
  3. Top-Tier Collaborations: Fostering strategic collaborations between classified hotels, tourism bodies, and government ministries.
  4. Statewide Representation: Unifying three-star, four-star, five-star, and heritage hotels across all twenty-six districts.
