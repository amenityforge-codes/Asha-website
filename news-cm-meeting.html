Official Meeting / Government Engagement

ASHA Representatives Meet the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh

Association: Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) Provisional Date: 26 June 2026 Location: Vijayawada / Amaravati

Official Engagement Summary

A delegation representing the Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) met the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh. The supplied photographs document the meeting and presentation materials. The accompanying Telugu newspaper clipping provides additional media context.

During the engagement, the delegation presented official association materials and sector representations concerning the hospitality and tourism infrastructure of Andhra Pradesh, including initiatives focused on Amaravati and statewide star hotel operations.

The meeting underscores ASHA’s role as the apex industry body facilitating structured dialogue between classified hospitality establishments and the State Government.
