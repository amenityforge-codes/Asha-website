Official Meeting / Government Engagement & Social Welfare

Star Hotels Assn Donates ₹50 Lakh to Anna Canteen Initiative & Submits Hospitality Roadmap to CM Chandrababu Naidu

Association: Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) Date: 30 June 2024 Location: State Secretariat, Amaravati Welfare Contribution: ₹50,00,000 (Anna Canteen Initiative)

Official Engagement Summary

A high-level leadership delegation from the Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association (ASHA) met with Hon’ble Chief Minister Sri N. Chandrababu Naidu at the State Secretariat in Amaravati. During the audience, the association contributed ₹50 lakh towards the State Government’s flagship Anna Canteen initiative and formally submitted the association’s official brochure and sectoral representation outlining the hospitality development roadmap for Amaravati and Andhra Pradesh.

The ₹50 lakh cheque was handed over directly to the Chief Minister by ASHA President R. V. Swamy alongside senior office bearers and prominent industry stalwarts. The contribution underscores the organized hospitality sector's tangible commitment to partnering with the State Government in social welfare, food security, and community upliftment for economically weaker sections.

Hon’ble Chief Minister Sri N. Chandrababu Naidu commended the Star Hotels Association for its proactive corporate citizenship and generous contribution to the Anna Canteen initiative. The Chief Minister highlighted that the hospitality and tourism sectors are cornerstone growth engines for Andhra Pradesh, and reiterated the government's commitment to creating an industry-friendly regulatory environment that accelerates five-star hotel investments across Amaravati, Visakhapatnam, and religious tourism corridors.

Alongside the welfare contribution, the delegation presented the association's official document, Amaravati Hospitality Roadmap, detailing member star properties statewide, bed capacity projections, and policy recommendations on power tariffs, luxury bar licensing, and municipal rationalization to support world-class infrastructure in Andhra Pradesh.

The Hans India: Star Hotels Assn donates to Anna canteen initiative

Source: The Hans India · Hans News Service, Amaravati · Published 30 June 2024

Verbatim Article Excerpt:

“MEMBERS of the Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association met Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu at the State Secretariat here on Monday and contributed Rs 50 lakh towards the Anna Canteen initiative. The association presented a cheque for Rs 50 lakh to the Chief Minister as part of its contribution to support the government's flagship programme, which provides affordable meals to the needy.”

“Appreciating the gesture, Naidu thanked the association members for their contribution and commended their commitment to supporting social welfare initiatives.”

“The delegation included AP Star Hotels Association President and Hyatt Place Chairman RV Swamy, Vice-President Pawan Karthik, Secretary C Murthy, Taj Vivanta Chairman Malineni Rajaiah, Murali Fortune Hotel Chairman Muttavarapu Murali, and GRT Hotels Chairman V Sharan.”
