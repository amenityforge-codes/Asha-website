Representation on ITC for GST Submitted to Hon'ble Tourism Minister Kandula Durgesh

Association: Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) Date: 8 October 2025 Location: Vijayawada

Executive Briefing & Submission Scope

The Andhra Pradesh Star Hotels Association submitted a representation concerning Input Tax Credit (ITC) under GST to Hon'ble Tourism Minister Kandula Durgesh. The newspaper report discusses GST-related concerns affecting the hospitality sector, including hotels and restaurants, and requests consideration of the industry's representation.

The delegation representing the association included President R. V. Swamy, General Secretary S. Murthy Chittoory, and Council Member Sharan V., who met the Hon'ble Minister in Vijayawada to provide a comprehensive sectoral briefing on the fiscal realities facing hospitality establishments across Andhra Pradesh.

The core submission urged the adoption of a 5% GST rate with Input Tax Credit (ITC) for hotels and restaurants. The association noted that hospitality remains virtually the only major consumer-facing service industry excluded from claiming input tax credits on operational inputs, construction, and capital refurbishments.

The submission was presented for the consideration of the State Government to support taking up the representation with the GST Council and Union Finance Ministry. This entry documents the submission of an industry representation; policy determinations and statutory rate adjustments remain subject to central and state legislative processes.

Regional Press Coverage

Source: Bharat News · Vijayawada Dateline · Published 8 October 2025

Newspaper clipping from Bharat News detailing the Star Hotels Association's representation on GST and Input Tax Credit.

Six Key Representation Points Documented in the Report:
  1. Working Capital: 5% GST without ITC inflates operating costs and locks up working capital.
  2. Capex & Fit-Outs: Capital costs on hotel construction and renovation rise effectively (~15%) due to blocked credits.
  3. SME Competitive Parity: Local employment-generating hotels are disadvantaged while industrial packaged food manufacturers continue to avail ITC.
  4. State Recommendation: Urged State advocacy to the Union Finance Ministry and GST Council for 5% GST with ITC across India.
  5. Investment Revival: Restoring ITC can revive stalled hotel projects and expand total tax throughput.
  6. Tourism Growth Engine: Aligns with Andhra Pradesh's focus on expanding MICE, destination weddings, and long-stay business travel.

Regional press coverage is archived for contextual media reference. Specific statements and summaries in press reports reflect the industry's submission; statutory policy determinations are subject to official Government and GST Council gazette notifications.
