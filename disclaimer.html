An application is not membership

Sending the form on the Membership page is a request to be considered. It does not create membership, a listing in the directory, or a right to use the Association's name or mark.

Eligibility

Hotel membership is open to properties classified as three-star and above in Andhra Pradesh. Industry and institutional partners may apply in the categories stated on the membership page. Meeting the description does not guarantee acceptance.

Review

The secretariat reviews applications. The steps, documents, fees, and code of conduct will be published when they are supplied.
