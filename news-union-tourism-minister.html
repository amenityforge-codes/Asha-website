ASHA Delegation Meets Hon'ble Union Tourism Minister

Senior office bearers and council members of ASHA convened with the Union Tourism Minister in Vijayawada to present industry representations and discuss central initiatives for Andhra Pradesh's hospitality sector.

Association: Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) Date: 26 June 2025 Time: Official Ministerial Consultation Location: Vijayawada / Andhra Pradesh

Official Ministerial Engagement Scope

A high-level leadership delegation from the Star Hotels Association of Andhra Pradesh (ASHA) met the Hon'ble Union Tourism Minister during his official visit to Vijayawada on 26 June 2025. The delegation formally felicitated the central leadership with a floral presentation and submitted comprehensive representations regarding the strategic growth of Andhra Pradesh's hospitality industry.

The engagement provided an authoritative platform for senior hoteliers to articulate state-level priorities directly before central policymaking authorities, focusing on key areas that impact hotel viability, regional tourism circuits, and national destination branding.

Delegation Representation

The ASHA leadership delegation was led by executive committee members and regional hotel directors, including:

  • R. V. Swamy: President, ASHA & Chairman, Hyatt Place Vijayawada
  • Ghattamaneni Venkat: Vice-President, ASHA & Joint MD, Daspalla Group, Visakhapatnam
  • Gudisa Shivakumar: Treasurer, ASHA & MD, SVN Lake Palace, Vizianagaram
  • Sharan Valluru: Executive Member, ASHA & MD, GRT Grand, Vijayawada
  • Executive Council Colleagues & Regional Hoteliers: Representing classified star hotels across Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Tirupati, Guntur, and Rajamahendravaram.

Strategic Discussion Points

Key topics submitted during the consultation included:

  1. Central Tourism Infrastructure: Leveraging central funding under Swadesh Darshan 2.0 and PRASHAD schemes to develop heritage, pilgrimage, and coastal circuits across Andhra Pradesh's 26 districts.
  2. Air & Rail Connectivity: Advocating enhanced direct air routes connecting Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, and Tirupati with major domestic business hubs and international gateways.
  3. Fiscal Policy Alignment: Presenting industry recommendations regarding Input Tax Credit (ITC) under GST for hotels and restaurants to incentivize ongoing facility upgrades and renovations.
  4. State-Center Tourism Synergies: Fostering joint promotional campaigns between the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, and the Andhra Pradesh Tourism Department to position the State as an attractive investment and MICE destination.
