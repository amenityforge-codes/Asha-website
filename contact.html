For effective government liaison, either the President or the General Secretary is ordinarily based in Vijayawada or Amaravati. Membership is open to hotels classified as three-star and above in Andhra Pradesh.

Institutional liaison

The Association coordinates with government departments, Andhra Pradesh Tourism authorities, local authorities, and other institutions from Vijayawada or Amaravati.

Until the registered office is notified, enquiries from member hotels, departments, and partners may be recorded through this page.

The Association does not exist for the private profit of its members. Correspondence concerning commercial services of individual hotels should be directed to those hotels.

A non-profit society representing three-star and above hotels across Andhra Pradesh.
