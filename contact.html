Secretariat Office

  • Liaison Headquarters

    Vijayawada / Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh

  • Official Correspondence

    Official communication is routed through the central secretariat registry. Direct contact details will be notified to accredited member properties and statutory departments upon formal notification.

  • Representation Scope

    Classified 3-Star, 4-Star, 5-Star, and Deluxe/Heritage hotels across all 26 districts of Andhra Pradesh.

  • Government & Departmental Coordination

    The Association coordinates with Andhra Pradesh Tourism, state government departments, municipal corporations, and statutory authorities from Vijayawada and Amaravati.

The Association serves the collective institutional interests of its members. Correspondence concerning room reservations or commercial services must be directed to individual hotel properties.
