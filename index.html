Mission

Represent, strengthen, and advance three-star and above hotels across Andhra Pradesh through excellence, safety, tourism-friendly policy, and professional development.
  • Hospitality excellence
  • Representation of 3-star and above hotels
  • Tourism-friendly public policy
  • High standards of service, hygiene, and food safety
  • Sustainability
  • Guest and operational safety
  • Professional development
  • A premier tourism destination

Aims

Five public commitments that give effect to the Mission. Clause numbers map to the Memorandum of Association.

3.3.1

Representation

Support and represent member hotels in public policy, investment, tourism development, industry regulation, and public engagement.

3.3.2

Collaboration

A unified platform among government, private stakeholders, educational institutions, and the hospitality industry.

3.3.3

Standards

Service quality, hygiene, food safety, guest and operational safety, professionalism, and sustainability.

3.3.4

Destination

Promote Andhra Pradesh as a premier tourism and hospitality destination through advocacy, training, and recognition.

3.3.5

Development

Growth, collaboration, professional excellence, and sustainable development of the organised hotel sector.

Objectives

In furtherance of the Mission and Aims, and not otherwise, the Association is authorised to pursue twelve Objectives.

3.5.1

Representation and Advocacy

Collective voice before government, tourism authorities, and regulators across 26 districts.

 3.5.2

Quality and Standards

Service excellence, hygiene, food safety, security, sustainability, and professional conduct.

 3.5.3

Training and Recognition

Programmes, workshops, continuous professional development, and industry awards.

 3.5.4

Crisis Support

Collective response during disasters, emergencies, regulatory change, and disruption.

 3.5.5

Tourism Promotion

National and international platforms, fairs, media engagement, and the positive image of member hotels.

 3.5.6

Institutional Affiliations

State, national, and international hospitality, tourism, trade, and professional bodies.

 3.5.7

Education and Research

Training institutions, skill development, laboratories, and hospitality innovation.

 3.5.8

Publications

Journals, reports, regulatory updates, and communication with members and the public.

 3.5.9

Events and Exhibitions

Conferences, conventions, trade exhibitions, and industry forums.

 3.5.10

Regional Development

Branches, chapters, and balanced participation across the State.

 3.5.11

Property and Resources

Lawful holding of assets and fundraising solely for Association objects.

 3.5.12

Incidental Powers

Lawful activities ancillary to the Aims, without private profit to members.

A unified platform

The Association represents the hospitality industry and facilitates cooperation among member hotels and the public and private bodies that govern, invest in, and educate the sector.
