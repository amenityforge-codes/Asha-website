What do we do

ASHA represents star hotels on shared industry matters, develops hospitality talent, convenes members and partners, and promotes Andhra Pradesh as a hospitality destination. The full Mission, Aims, and Objectives live under About ASHA.

  1. Policy representation and regulatory advocacy

    Coordinated regulatory updates, representation on common industry matters, and participation in government consultations.

  2. Training and workforce development

    Access to training programmes and professional development for hospitality talent.

  3. Industry networking and knowledge sharing

    A platform among member hotels, government, private stakeholders, and educational institutions.

  4. Tourism promotion and destination development

    Visibility through ASHA's tourism-promotion initiatives and advocacy for Andhra Pradesh as a hospitality destination.
