Tangible Value

How ASHA serves the hospitality sector

ASHA delivers concrete institutional representation, policy advocacy, operational benchmarks, and destination visibility for member properties statewide.

  1. Policy Representation & Regulatory Advocacy

    Direct institutional representation before Andhra Pradesh Tourism, state ministries, municipal corporations, and regulators on statutory compliance, licenses, taxation, and sectoral incentives.

  2. Standards & Operational Excellence

    Advancing service benchmarks, safety protocols, sustainable practices, and operational standards across classified star hotels throughout the State.

  3. Institutional Coordination & Crisis Support

    A coordinated industry platform for collective representation during regulatory revisions, policy consultations, emergencies, and state tourism master planning.

  4. Destination Promotion & Visibility

    Strategic alignment with Andhra Pradesh Tourism's event calendar and state campaigns to showcase star hotel capacity and drive domestic and international visitor traffic.

Mutual Value

Why engage with ASHA

Delivering clear, tangible outcomes for hospitality operators and public institutions alike.

Why Star Hotels Join ASHA

A Heavyweight Seat at the Table: Individual hotels face significant hurdles navigating complex municipal rules, utility tariffs, and licensing mandates alone. ASHA gives member properties collective representation and direct access to state policymakers.

Early Regulatory Insights: Stay ahead of upcoming compliance guidelines, star re-classification standards, and state tourism subsidy schemes.

Executive Leadership Network: Connect directly with owners, general managers, and decision-makers shaping hospitality across all 26 districts.

Explore Membership Benefits →

Why Government Bodies Engage with ASHA

A Single Authoritative Interlocutor: Government departments and Andhra Pradesh Tourism interact with one accredited, responsible association representing star hotels statewide, rather than fragmented individual properties.

Ground-Level Policy Feedback: Reliable industry consultations, operational data, and feasibility feedback for tourism master plans and infrastructure investments.

Standard-Bearers of Excellence: Institutional assurance that member properties uphold guest safety, hygiene, and the highest hospitality benchmarks under Atithi Devo Bhava.
